What would our
lives really look like?

◆

I tried looking into our tomorrow.

And I wouldn't lie and say it was perfect.

◆

There were downsides

  • What if I ended up breaking your heart, or you mine?
  • What if we never stayed together long enough to step into the world of Forever?
  • What if after knowing each other's flaws, we never look each other the same?
  • What if you were meant to find a better man than I, and I another woman?
  • What if we divorced before or during our Forever?
  • What if...?
◆

"What if" is all it was. Yet to me, the upsides held more weight.

  • Mine & Your imaginations about us. The hope that we could be the best versions of ourselves together.

Since, Faith is the belief in things not yet seen, and so are our imaginations. It's easier for me to have hope in what God has in store for us two. I wouldn't want to go against His will for you. But I am willing to trust that everything is going according to His will, and if He chooses to bless this-us, then Forever it will be.

◆

I understand your concerns. You have a side of you, you want to work on - probably alone. And I want nothing more than to be the kind of man you can trust and look up to. But I too need a lot of working on.

◆

I know relationships aren't easy. A responsibility I am willing to take on. Only if you want me to.

◆

If after reading this, it doesn't feel right to you. Don't feel bad.

◆

You saying Yes to me out of pity will do me more harm than saying No. You saying Yes on the other hand, out of mutual consent, will give me a chance of winning something precious for the first time in a long time.

◆

That will be You!

◆

I am going to have you make a decision, but I want you to know what you're getting into so you make the right, even maybe — the most logical choice.

◆

I always try to apologise when I feel I made you uncomfortable, yet you always seem to be unbothered. Seemingly already understanding my flow of thoughts, the words I say, and the words I keep.

I try to predict the thoughts of others, sometimes out of concern and sometimes out of distrust. Yet you understand them, and even the heart behind it.

◆

Even still, I apologise for putting you in this state, as I ask you to be the wife of my youth, and if God wills it, someday, the wife of my older days to come.

◆

Then this is us.

If the Lord wills, let Him bless this for us two as we begin this journey of mind, heart and soul.

Thank you for saying yes.❤️

◆

I understand.

You saying no does me no harm. Thank you for reading this with care, and for being honest with me.

I wish you a life that is kind to you, and our friendship will in no way be affected by this.😌

◆

I hope it wasn't boring.

Take your time before you answer...
I'll be right here, whatever your decision may be.
